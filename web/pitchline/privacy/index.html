Pitchline Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Pitchline (“the app”) is a singing pitch app for Android and iOS, published by Study Music Theory. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

The short version: everything stays on your device. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access so that it can show your pitch in real time, record your voice, and record you singing along with a song (“takes”). Microphone audio is processed on your device. It is never uploaded or sent to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

  • Your recordings and takes, and songs you choose to import (with the vocal and music the app separates from them on your device).
  • Your songs’ details: the notes found in them, key, transpose and harmony choices.
  • Your settings.

This data lives in the app’s private storage on your device. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, and it is not synced anywhere by the app.

Network use

The app works offline. It uses the network only the first time you import a song: it then downloads a vocal separation model file (about 20 MB) once, from Hugging Face or studymusictheory.com. Your songs are never uploaded or stored on a server. The request sends no information about you or your audio, other than what any web request contains, such as your IP address. The model then runs entirely on your device.

Exports and sharing

When you export audio or video, the file is created on your device and handed to your device’s share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you and the app you choose.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Privacy and data → Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app. See how to delete your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Study Music Theory support page.
