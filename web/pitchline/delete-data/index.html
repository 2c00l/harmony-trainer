Delete your Pitchline data

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Pitchline has no accounts and stores no data on our servers. Your recordings, songs, takes and settings exist only on your device, so you can delete them yourself at any time.

Delete everything from inside the app

  1. Open Pitchline.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon.
  3. Scroll to Privacy and data and tap Delete all my data.
  4. Confirm. All songs, recordings, takes and settings are permanently removed.

Delete a single song or take

Tap the folder icon (My songs) and tap the bin next to a song. Inside a song you can also tap ⋯ → Delete song. To delete one of your sing-along recordings, tap Takes and the bin next to it.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data from your device. On Android you can instead use Settings → Apps → Pitchline → Storage → Clear storage.

Data held by us

None. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have questions, contact us through the support page.
