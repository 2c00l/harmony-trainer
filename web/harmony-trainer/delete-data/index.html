Delete your Harmony Trainer data

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Harmony Trainer has no accounts and stores no data on our servers. Your recordings, projects, scores and settings exist only on your device, so you can delete them yourself at any time.

Delete everything from inside the app

  1. Open Harmony Trainer.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon.
  3. Scroll to Privacy and data and tap Delete all my data.
  4. Confirm. All projects, recordings, scores, streaks, calibration and settings are permanently removed.

Delete a single project or layer

On the Studio screen, tap ⋯ next to a project and choose Delete. Inside a project, open Mix, tap ⋯ on a layer and choose Delete layer.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data from your device. On Android you can instead use Settings → Apps → Harmony Trainer → Storage → Clear storage.

Data held by us

None. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have questions, contact us through the support page.
