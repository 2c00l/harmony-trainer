## Full description (both stores)
Pitchline shows your voice as you sing. Every note you sing draws a glowing line across a keyboard, so you can see straight away whether you're in tune, sharp or flat.

SEE YOUR PITCH, LIVE
• Your voice drawn in real time, with the note name and how many cents sharp or flat you are
• Pick a key and its notes light up on the keyboard
• Steady, calm display that ignores breaths and stray noises

SING ALONG WITH YOUR SONGS
• Import a song from your phone and Pitchline takes the vocal out of it, right on your device
• The melody appears as notes you can follow, with the original vocal and the music
• Start anywhere: drag the notes, use the slider or skip back and forward

SING THE HARMONY
• Pitchline writes a harmony part for any melody: 3rds, 4ths, 5ths, 6ths, octaves or unison, above or below
• Stay in key, or follow the melody's exact shape
• Move the harmony an octave up or down to suit your voice

TRANSPOSE TO SUIT YOUR VOICE
• Move the whole song up or down without changing its speed
• Notes, harmony and key move with it

RECORD YOURSELF
• Record a melody of your own and get a harmony for it
• Sing along with a song and keep your takes
• Hear yourself back with the song and see your pitch line next to the notes

SHARE
• Export audio (M4A or WAV) or a portrait video of the notes scrolling with the sound

MADE FOR EVERY SINGER
• Large and extra-large text, high contrast, colour-blind safe colours
• Screen reader support with spoken notes, reduce motion, left-handed layout
• Keyboard shortcuts and swipe gestures
• Note names as letters, Do Re Mi or numbers

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything happens on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your songs and recordings are never uploaded or stored on a server.

Pitchline is made by Study Music Theory.
